# HookLab.AI launch kit

Ready-to-use copy for getting the first 100 creators. Pair it with
[the launch plan](LAUNCH_PLAN.md). Live app: https://hooklab-ai.vercel.app

## Positioning

**One line:** Paste your video idea, get 10 scroll-stopping opening lines
for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, in English, Hinglish or Hindi. Free, no sign-up.

**Who first:** Indian short-form creators with 1k–50k followers who post in
Hinglish or Hindi. Most hook tools only write English; that gap is the wedge.

**What makes it different from ChatGPT:**

- Ten named hook frameworks at once, not one generic line.
- Each hook comes with on-screen text and a first-shot idea.
- A speaking-time check and a practice timer: does it fit in 5 seconds?
- Roast mode grades a hook you already wrote; Compare picks between two.

**Never claim** guaranteed views, virality or retention. Scores are AI
estimates. Say "stronger openings", not "go viral".

## The first week: free hook audits

This is the highest-value activity at zero users. Aim for 5 audits a day.

1. Pick one niche (for example study/UPSC, fitness, personal finance, tech).
2. Find creators with 1k–50k followers who post at least weekly.
3. Pick one recent video with a weak opening. Run its opening through
   HookLab (Roast mode), and choose the two best rewrites.
4. Send the DM below. Never paste the link first; lead with the value.
5. Log each DM: handle, niche, replied?, used a hook?, visited?

## Daily content: hook teardowns

Post one short vertical video a day on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts,
recorded as a screen capture of HookLab. Structure (about 30 seconds):

1. **0–3s:** show the weak opening on screen. Say: "This hook loses people
   in 2 seconds. Here's why."
2. **3–10s:** name the one problem (vague, starts with "I", no tension).
3. **10–22s:** paste it into HookLab, show two rewrites and pick one.
4. **22–27s:** open "Practice saying it" and show it fits the 5s window.
5. **27–30s:** "Free tool, link in bio."

Use real outputs from the app on camera. The pairs below are starting ideas
for the weak openings; generate the rewrites live.

| #   | Weak opening to tear down                                     | Angle to show            |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| 1   | "In this video I'll share my top tips for productivity."      | Vague promise → specific |
| 2   | "Aaj main aapko batane wala hoon ki paise kaise bachayein."   | Hinglish, slow start     |
| 3   | "I've been doing this for 5 years and here's what I learned." | Starts with "I"          |
| 4   | "Hey guys, welcome back to my channel!"                       | Wasted first 2 seconds   |
| 5   | "Is this the best budget phone of 2026?"                      | Weak question → stakes   |

Put the link in bio: https://hooklab-ai.vercel.app

## What to measure

Vercel Analytics already records these events without any script or hook
text: `hooks_generated`, `hook_copied`, `hook_shared`, `hook_saved`,
`practice_finished`, `shared_link_opened`. (Custom events need a Vercel plan
that includes them; page views work on every plan.)

The number that matters most: **creators who copy or share a hook and come
back within 7 days.** Visits alone don't tell you the product works.
